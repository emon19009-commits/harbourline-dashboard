## Approval thresholds

| Impact | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to AUD 2,000 and up to 2 days, no change to objectives | Project manager |
| Up to AUD 10,000 and up to 1 week | Change Control Board |
| Above AUD 10,000 or more than 1 week, or any change to objectives or go-live date | Sponsor |
| Any use of the contingency reserve | Sponsor |
